It can be [customized](/en/docs/sizing-icons) or added as your need. Tailwind utilities like [size](https://tailwindcss.com/docs/size), [height](https://tailwindcss.com/docs/height) and [width](https://tailwindcss.com/docs/width) can be used as well.
